## Overview

Noemi Halvorsen is a Principal Research Engineer at Tessellate Labs in Lisbon. She works on efficient machine-learning systems, data infrastructure, and large-scale retrieval, and leads the lab's inference-systems group.

## Experience

- **Principal Research Engineer, Tessellate Labs** (2023-present): leads the inference-systems group and ships low-latency model serving and data pipelines for media and logistics clients.
- **Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Corvane Analytics** (2019-2023): built feature stores and ranking services and owned the experiment platform.
- **Data Engineer, Bluefin Systems** (2016-2019): designed batch and streaming ingestion for telemetry.

## Selected Work

See the [field reports](field-reports.md) page. Recent work includes *Triton-Kernel Autotuning for Sparse Attention at Scale* and *Vectorized Reranking with Learned Quantization*.

## Competencies

- **Inference systems:** Triton kernel autotuning, PyTorch optimisation, Kubernetes deployment.
- **Retrieval and data:** FAISS vector search, NumPy, SQL, dbt pipelines, Spark batch and stream processing.
- **Observability:** Prometheus metrics and Grafana dashboards.
